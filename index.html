About us

Our mission and vision for the community.

Mission

To ignite scientific curiosity and foster active learning in astronomy, astrophysics, and space technology by promoting student-led research, interdisciplinary collaboration, and events that connect theory with practice.

Vision

To become a leading student-driven platform that inspires space exploration, promotes scientific thinking, and bridges academia with space research in India.

We host workshops, competitions, and celebrations that bring students and space enthusiasts together.
